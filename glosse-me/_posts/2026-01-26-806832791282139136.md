> # The Secret History Translation Masterlist
>
> i was going to put this on a spam account but then decided to put it on a public one. who knows, maybe someone will benefit from it! if i’ve made any mistakes, do let me know
>
> 1. *à* *moi. l’histoire d’une de mes folies* (to me. the history of one of my follies **or** my turn. the tale of my madness)
> 2. *quod erat demonstrandum* (it can be shown)
> 3. *cubitum eamus?* (will you sleep with me?)
> 4. *consummatum est* (it is done)
> 5. *hoi polloi. barbaroi* [the many/majority. barbarian (person who doesn’t speak greek)]
> 6. *bei nacht und nebel* (at night and in fog)
> 7. *deprendi miserum est* (it is wretched to be found out)
> 8. *khairei* (hello)
> 9. *bakchoi* (initiates)
> 10. *cuniculus molestus* (annoying rabbit)
> 11. *arrectis auribus* (attentively/ears peeled)
> 12. *dormir plutôt que vivre* (sleep rather than live)
> 13. *dans un sommeil aussi doux que la mort* (in a sleep as sweet as death)
> 14. *requiescat in pace* (rest in peace)
> 15. *n’est-ce pas* (isn’t that so)
> 16. *amor vincit omnia* (love conquers all)
> 17. *raison d’être* (reason for existence)
> 18. *nihil sub sole novum* (there is nothing new under the sun)
> 19. *quel plaisir de vous revoir* (what a great pleasure to see you again)
> 20. *genis gratus, corpore glabellus, arte multiscius, et fortuna opulentus* (smooth-cheeked, soft-skinned, well-educated and rich)
> 21. *dénouement* (outcome)
> 22. *salve, amice* (hello, friend)
> 23. *valesne?* (are you well?)
> 24. *quid est rei?* (what is the matter?)
> 25. *benigne dicis* (i thank you)
> 26. *bureau de tabac* (tobacco store)
> 27. *Χαλεπά τά καλά* (beauty is harsh)
> 28. *mais, vrai, j’ai trop pleuré!* (oh, truly, i have wept too much!)
> 29. *les aubes sont navrantes* (the dawns are heartbreaking)
> 30. *hinc illae lacrimae* (hence those tears)
> 31. *sic oculos, sic ille manus, sic ora ferebat* (such eyes, such hands, such looks)
